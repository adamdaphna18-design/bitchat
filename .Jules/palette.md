## 2026-06-01 - Custom Media Controls and Icon-only Buttons
**Learning:** Custom SwiftUI media components (like waveforms or icon-only cancel/play buttons) lack implicit accessibility context, meaning VoiceOver users get no state feedback. Applying `.accessibilityElement(children: .ignore)` alongside dynamic `.accessibilityLabel` and `.accessibilityValue` is crucial for custom drawn shapes, while simple plain English labels wrapped in `String(localized: "...", comment: "...")` work best for interactive icon buttons to ensure Xcode String Catalogs correctly pick them up.
**Action:** Always verify that every custom icon-only button and drawn media control (e.g. Canvas/Waveform) has explicitly configured, conditionally-dynamic `.accessibilityLabel` or `.accessibilityValue` modifiers to reflect current state.
